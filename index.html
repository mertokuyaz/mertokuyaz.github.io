Bitcoin Mining Simulator

An interactive tool to understand how Proof-of-Work mining works.

Created by x.com/mertokuyazz

Did you know? In 2009, 3 hours of mining on a home PC could have earned ~100 BTC, worth over $5 Million USD today.
The 'Extreme' setting here is inspired by that era but is made *much* easier so you can actually find a block and understand how it works!
